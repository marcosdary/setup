01 My story
2013 - 2017

The beginning: Computer Engineering at Ceuma University

My journey in technology began during my Computer Engineering degree at Ceuma University. That was when I had my first real contact with programming after being encouraged to study Python through Curso em Video.

From there, I immersed myself in the language ecosystem, exploring everything from fundamentals to areas such as data analysis and automation. More than learning a stack, I started to understand how to solve problems in a structured way.

During college, I developed a strong interest in algorithms and data structures. Not because of the purely academic side, but because of the practical impact: the right choice can be the difference between a system that scales and one that fails under load.

2018 - 2020

First job: learning in the real world

As happens to many people at the beginning of their careers, entering the job market required persistence. Through attempts, rejections, and continuous learning, I understood that real growth comes from building.

During that time, I started building my own projects, working as a freelancer, and exploring SaaS product ideas. That was where I consolidated important foundations: autonomy, technical responsibility, and product thinking.

02 How I work
// 01
Simplicity before elegance

Code that anyone can understand quickly is more valuable than complex solutions that are hard to maintain.

// 02
Resilient systems by design

Failures are inevitable. The question is not "if", but "when". Systems should be designed to keep operating even under failure.

// 03
Observability from day one

Logs, metrics, and traces are part of the system, not optional extras. Without visibility, there is no evolution.

// 04
Communication as a technical skill

Explaining technical decisions clearly is just as important as making them. Communication is engineering too.

03 Outside the terminal

"Designing Data-Intensive Applications" is one of the books that most shaped the way I think about systems. Outside technology, I enjoy science fiction, especially authors like Asimov, Liu Cixin, and Kim Stanley Robinson.
